gavel Regulation Directive 04-B

Community Rules

Adherence to these guidelines ensures a high-quality roleplay experience for all operators within the Verge-il sector. Ignorance is not a valid defense.

priority_high
Last Updated
OCT 24, 2024

01. General Conduct

Harassment, hate speech, or excessive toxicity toward any community member is strictly prohibited. Verge-il is an inclusive high-stakes environment where rivalries remain in-character. Any Out-of-Character (OOC) toxicity will result in an immediate permanent ban.
Using third-party software, macros, or exploiting game engine bugs for a competitive advantage is forbidden. This includes crosshairs not provided by the server, "powergaming" mechanical systems, and lag switching.

02. Roleplay Quality

CRITICAL psychology

Powergaming (PG)

Performing actions that are physically impossible or forcing roleplay outcomes on others without giving them a chance to react. Your character is not a superhero.

CRITICAL account_tree

Metagaming (MG)

Using information your character would not know in-game (Stream info, Discord logs, OOC chat) to influence IC decisions. Keep it separate.

MANDATORY emergency

Value of Life (NVL)

You must act as if your character's life is their most precious asset. Running into a group of 5 armed men with only a knife is NVL and reportable.

MANDATORY sensor_occupied

Character Integrity

Stay in character at all times. If a rule break occurs, finish the scenario and report it later. Do not break immersion to complain.

03. Combat & Conflict

3.1

Random Deathmatch (RDM)

Attacking or killing another player without a valid, high-quality roleplay reason and prior initiation. "I wanted your car" is not initiation.

3.2

Vehicle Deathmatch (VDM)

Using a vehicle as a primary weapon to ram or kill players. Vehicles should only be used for transportation or tactical cover.

3.3

Combat Logging

Disconnecting from the server to avoid a combat situation, police arrest, or medical roleplay. Log checks are automatic.

04. Enforcement Policy

security

Staff Authority

Verge-il administrators have final say in all disputes. We follow a "Three Strikes" policy for minor infractions, while major violations result in permanent de-authorization.
